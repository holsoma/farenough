## In the app

fairenough stores passes, journeys, fare assessments, saved route fares, import history and preferences in the app's private storage on your Android device. It does not require an account. It has no analytics, advertising, location permission or internet permission.

Automatic Android cloud backup and device-transfer extraction are excluded for these records. Use the explicit backup and restore controls to move them. Uninstalling the app removes its local records.

## Files you choose to export

Backups and CSV exports are unencrypted files. You choose their destination using Android's file picker. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider may upload the file according to its own settings. The app does not choose that destination for you.

## External LLMs

The formatting prompt is copied only when you press its button. The app does not contact an LLM. If you paste your travel records into another service, that service's data practices apply. You decide what to share.

## This website

The site has no analytics or advertising scripts. Fonts are served with the site. GitHub Pages and GitHub Releases handle website visits and downloads under GitHub's privacy practices.

## Questions

Use the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/holsoma/fairenough) to report an issue. Avoid posting your private travel history in a public issue.
