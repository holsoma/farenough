## How many more journeys?

Open **How many more trips?** on Overview, or **View pass result** once its cost is covered. The average-fare scenario divides the remaining amount by your average priced fare, then rounds up. Zero-fare journeys count in the average. Unpriced journeys do not, and the result states that coverage is incomplete.

The route scenario uses the automatic fare for the chosen stations and date within the selected pass period, or a saved directional override. A zero or unknown fare cannot produce a meaningful remaining trip count. The answer is a scenario, not a prediction of your future travel.

## Choose the period

Insights defaults to the selected pass. Switch to **Calendar month** to inspect one month instead. Counts, charts, rankings and the map share that filter.

## Charts

The cumulative chart compares recorded fare value with the pass-price line when viewing a pass period. The daily chart shows how many trips were recorded on each date. Select a point, bar or row in the daily values list to see the journeys behind it.

## Rail fare distance

Insights adds the rail fare distances of covered journeys and states how many journeys are included. It updates with the chosen pass period or calendar month. This is LTA's charging distance and can differ from the route you took. Dates outside the bundled coverage are excluded from this distance total.

## Most travelled route

Opposite directions rank separately. Toa Payoh → Bugis and Bugis → Toa Payoh are different routes. Equal counts share a rank. Each route shows both trip count and priced-record coverage.

## Singapore map

The map uses bundled coastline and station locations. It works offline and needs no location permission. Thicker connections represent more journeys. Arrows show direction. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and choose **Reset map** to return to the island view.

Selecting a connection or route-list row shows that route's recorded journeys. The lines connect entry and exit stations; they do not claim to show your precise train path. The station count includes distinct start/end stations only.


To browse older imports, select **Calendar month**, then **Choose month**. The list shows each recorded month and its journey count. Pick a month to update Charts, Map and Routes together. You can still use Previous and Next to inspect months without records. Calendar views do not require a saved pass.


## Compare calendar months

Open **Insights → Compare**. Choose the month to inspect and a second recorded month. The app shows the selected month's recorded fares, its difference from the other month, journey counts, travel days and average fares. Tap a month in **Month by month** to select it, or choose **Explore** to open that month's charts and map.

Switch the monthly bars between **Journeys** and **Fares**. Fare comparisons remain incomplete if either month has missing prices. Average fares use priced journeys only. A month still in progress, incomplete records, or a tariff change can affect the comparison. Calendar fare totals are not pass savings; pass comparisons continue to use each pass's actual validity dates.

The map labels the busiest stations. Select a connection to show both station names and its journey count above the map. Larger text uses wrapping view controls and stacked comparison values.
