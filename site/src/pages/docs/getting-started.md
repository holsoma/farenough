## Add your pass

Open farenough and choose **Set up a pass**. Enter the first and last valid days shown on your pass, including the year. Both dates count. The suggested end date is editable.

The default price is S$48 for an undergraduate train pass. Change it if the amount you paid differs. The comparison uses adult card fares, which are the applicable pay-per-trip fares for undergraduates.

The app supports one person's pass history. Pass periods cannot overlap. Use the pass button in the top bar to select a past period or add the next one.

## Add a journey

Press **+**, choose the travel date and your start and end stations, then save. A journey includes line changes inside the paid area. Each saved entry is treated as a separate paid journey.

Choosing two stations shows the regular adult fare and rail fare distance automatically for travel dates from **12 July through 25 December 2026**. If its fare is unavailable, the journey is still saved and marked **Fare needed** until you add a checked route fare or a correction.

## Check the result

Overview compares the priced journeys within the selected pass period against its price. It shows the amount remaining, exact break-even, or estimated savings above the pass price. An incomplete calculation states how many journeys have been priced.

You can bring in old records through **Trips → Import**. See [Importing your records](../importing/).
