## One entry, one exit

Choose your entry and exit stations by official name or station code. Internal train changes belong to that entry. If you exit and make another independently charged journey, record a new trip.

The first version supports MRT and LRT. Bus journeys and automatic transfer grouping are outside its scope.

## Usual journeys

Choose **Save as a favourite route** in the journey form. Your saved routes appear on Overview. Tap one to prefill the start and end stations, confirm the date and save. **Swap stations** prepares the return direction.

## Correct one fare

The optional corrected-fare field applies to that journey alone. Add a reason, such as a known morning discount. Leave the field blank to use the saved regular route fare. Zero is a valid correction for a qualifying free journey.

## Edit or delete

Tap a journey in Trips to open it. Changing its date or stations reassesses its regular fare for that route and date. A trip-specific correction remains until you change or remove it.

Deletion asks for confirmation and removes the journey from all counts, charts and maps. A full backup is useful before making extensive changes.
